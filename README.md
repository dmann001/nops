## Problem Statement
Traditional navigation technologies such as GPS, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth fail to provide accurate or reliable positioning in indoor, underground, or remote environments. This lack of offline navigation capability severely limits operational effectiveness in critical contexts such as:

Emergency services (e.g., disaster response in collapsed or remote areas)

Military and defense operations (requiring stealth and reliability without GPS signals)

Indoor robotics (e.g., warehouse automation or search-and-rescue robots)
